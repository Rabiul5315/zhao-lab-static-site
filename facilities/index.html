Facilities

Instrumentation and lab spaces supporting the lab's protein functionality, flavor chemistry, and sensory research.

Gas Chromatography–Olfactometry–Mass Spectrometry (GC-O-MS)

Agilent 8890 GC with 5977C MSD and a Gerstel ODP olfactory detection port for aroma-compound separation, identification, and sniff-port analysis.

High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC)

Shimadzu LC-2060C 3D integrated HPLC for separation and quantification of non-volatile compounds.

Amino Acid Analyzer

Hitachi L-8900. Analyzes amino acid composition of proteins and hydrolysates.

Protein / Nitrogen Analyzer

Elementar rapidN EXCEED. Determines total nitrogen and crude protein by the Dumas combustion method.

Mineral Analyzer (ICP-OES)

Agilent 5800 ICP-OES for multi-element mineral analysis of foods and ingredients.

Texture Analyzer

Stable Micro Systems TA.XTplus. Measures texture and mechanical properties such as hardness, gel strength, and fracturability.

Rotational Viscometer

Brookfield (AMETEK) rotational viscometer for viscosity measurement of solutions and dispersions.

High-Speed Centrifuge

Beckman Coulter Avanti JXN-26 floor-standing high-speed centrifuge for large-volume and higher-speed separations.

Refrigerated Centrifuge

Sorvall RT6000B refrigerated benchtop centrifuge.

Benchtop Centrifuge

Eppendorf 5810 R refrigerated benchtop centrifuge.

Soxtec Solvent Extraction System

FOSS ST 255. Semi-automated solvent extraction for crude fat, total fat, and extractable matter.

Thermal Extractor

Gerstel TE thermal extractor with C200 controller for releasing volatiles from solid samples ahead of GC-O-MS.

Tube Conditioner

Conditions adsorbent sample tubes used for volatile trapping and GC-O-MS analysis.

Freeze Dryer

Harvest Right freeze dryer with vacuum pump for lyophilizing foods, proteins, and other samples.

Vacuum Oven

Benchtop vacuum oven for low-temperature drying and moisture determination under reduced pressure.

Parr Multi-Reactor System

Parr 5000 Multi Reactor System with 4871 process controller for parallel stirred, heated pressure reactions.

Reaction Station

Radleys carousel reaction station on a Heidolph Hei-PLATE stirring hotplate for parallel small-scale reactions.

Ultrasonic Cell Disruptor (Sonicator)

Keshing KS-750F ultrasonic cell disruptor (probe sonicator) for cell disruption, dispersion, and emulsification.

Homogenizer

Rotor-stator homogenizer for particle-size reduction and dispersion of suspensions and emulsions.

Magnetic Stirrer

VELP Scientifica Multistirrer Digital multi-position magnetic stirrer.

Stirring Hotplate

Fisherbrand Isotemp digital stirring hotplate for heating and mixing.

Heat Block

VWR Digital Heatblock for temperature-controlled incubation of vials and tubes.

Water Bath

Fisherbrand Isotemp GPD 20 water bath for temperature-controlled sample incubation.

pH / Multiparameter Meter

Mettler Toledo SevenExcellence benchtop meter for pH and related multiparameter measurements.

Autoclave

Tuttnauer 2840EL benchtop autoclave for steam sterilization of media and labware.

Analytical Balance

Mettler Toledo ME54E analytical balance (0.1 mg readability) for precise gravimetric measurement.

Pipette Rack

Set of Eppendorf Research plus micropipettes on a carousel stand for accurate liquid handling.

Sensory Evaluation Booths

Five booths in PSFA 428, each with a sink and serving hatch, plus an adjoining food-prep kitchen (PSFA 424).

Sensory Evaluation Laboratory

The sensory laboratory (PSFA 428) has five booths, each equipped with a sink and a serving hatch. A kitchen / food-preparation room (PSFA 424) on the other side of the serving hatch has refrigerators, freezers, stoves, blenders, and other supplies used to store and prepare food for sensory studies. SDSU maintains a trained sensory panel, and test setup and analysis use RedJade software.
