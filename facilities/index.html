Facilities

Instrumentation and lab spaces supporting the lab's protein functionality, flavor chemistry, and sensory research. Managed in _data/facilities.yml.

Instrumentation

Instrumentation

Gas Chromatography–Olfactometry (GC-O)

San Diego State University

Identifying odor-active compounds in protein-rich foods.

Agilent 8890 GC with 5977C MSD and a Gerstel ODP 4 olfactory detection port, coupling instrumental separation with human sensory detection of aroma compounds.

Instrumentation

Soxtec Fat Extractor

San Diego State University

Measuring crude fat, total fat, and extractable matter.

FOSS ST 255. Semi-automated solvent extraction for crude fat, total fat, and extractable matter in food samples.

Instrumentation

Amino Acid Analyzer

San Diego State University

Determining amino acid composition of proteins.

Hitachi L-8900. Analyzes the amino acid composition of proteins and protein hydrolysates.

Lab Spaces

Lab Spaces

Sensory Evaluation Laboratory

PSFA 428 (booths) & PSFA 424 (food-prep kitchen)

Discrimination, descriptive, and consumer sensory testing.

Five booths, each with a sink and serving hatch, plus an adjoining food-preparation kitchen. SDSU maintains a trained sensory panel; test setup and analysis use RedJade software.

Sensory testing services

SDSU maintains a trained sensory panel. The table below summarizes the main test types the sensory laboratory supports.
