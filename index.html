Welcome

The Zhao Food Chemistry Lab at San Diego State University studies protein chemistry, flavor chemistry, alternative proteins, and data-driven tools for food science. Our research explores how food proteins and flavor compounds influence functionality, sensory quality, stability, and overall food performance.

By combining laboratory experiments, molecular-level analysis, curated datasets, and computational modeling, our lab aims to support the development of sustainable, nutritious, and high-quality protein-based foods.

Instrumentation & lab spaces

Instrumentation

Gas Chromatography–Olfactometry (GC-O)

San Diego State University

Identifying odor-active compounds in protein-rich foods.

Agilent 8890 GC with 5977C MSD and a Gerstel ODP 4 olfactory detection port, coupling instrumental separation with human sensory detection of aroma compounds.

Instrumentation

Soxtec Fat Extractor

San Diego State University

Measuring crude fat, total fat, and extractable matter.

FOSS ST 255. Semi-automated solvent extraction for crude fat, total fat, and extractable matter in food samples.

Instrumentation

Amino Acid Analyzer

San Diego State University

Determining amino acid composition of proteins.

Hitachi L-8900. Analyzes the amino acid composition of proteins and protein hydrolysates.
