Research Areas

Broad themes that organize the lab's work across food protein chemistry, flavor, alternative proteins, and data-driven tools.

Food Protein Functionality

How food proteins behave structurally and physically in real food systems.

We study how food proteins gel, hold water and oil, dissolve, emulsify, and foam — and how processing and formulation change those behaviors. This structure–function understanding guides the design of protein ingredients with reliable texture and performance.

  • Water- and oil-holding capacity
  • Solubility
  • Gelation & texture (TPA)
  • Emulsifying & foaming properties
  • Rheology

Alternative & Plant-Based Proteins

Novel proteins — plant, algae, and insect — for sustainable foods.

We characterize novel protein sources, including plant, algae, and insect proteins, and evaluate how they perform in alternative meat and other product formats. The goal is nutritious, functional, and sustainable protein ingredients.

  • Protein extraction & fractionation
  • Functional characterization
  • Alternative meat formulation

Flavor Chemistry of Protein-Rich Foods

Aroma and flavor compounds in meat, seafood, and protein ingredients.

Using gas chromatography–olfactometry and sensory analysis, we identify the odor-active compounds that shape the flavor of meat, seafood, and protein-rich ingredients, and study how they form and can be controlled.

  • Gas chromatography–olfactometry (GC-O)
  • GC–MS
  • Sensory evaluation

Algae-Based Ingredients

Microalgae ingredients and stabilization of the blue colorant C-phycocyanin.

We work on microalgae as a food ingredient, including the stabilization of C-phycocyanin — a natural blue colorant derived from spirulina — and the functional and sensory properties of algae-derived materials.

  • Colorant stabilization
  • Functional characterization
  • Sensory & allergen screening

Data-Driven & Computational Tools

Machine learning, deep learning, and molecular simulation for food proteins.

We build data-driven tools — machine learning, deep learning, and molecular dynamics simulation — to predict protein functionality and food quality and to accelerate ingredient design.

  • Machine learning & deep learning
  • Molecular dynamics simulation
  • Curated functionality datasets
